REN application · Malaysian

Prepare these documents

NCC certificate · Contract for Service · NRIC front and back · Passport photo · Education certificate · Recent bankruptcy report.

Generate the bankruptcy report only after signing your Contract for Service.
  1. Attend and pass the 2-day NCC with an LPEPH-appointed association.
  2. Send the NCC certificate to NA and complete the Contract for Service.
  3. Return all complete documents. NA submits through BIS LPEPH.
  4. NA informs you after approval and shares the business card order link.
REN application · Non-Malaysian

Prepare the NCC certificate, Contract for Service, passport main and visa pages, work permit, passport photo, Diploma or higher, bankruptcy report and Statutory Declaration.

Mandatory: Work permit must state “Real Estate Negotiator at PropNex Realty Sdn Bhd”. Only Resident Passes and Spouse Visas are accepted.

You apply for the work permit yourself. NA provides the supporting documents required by Immigration.

PEA application

All documents must be Certified True Copy by the Agency Principal.

Prepare the recognised real estate education certificate, Contract for Service, NRIC, birth certificate, transcript, Form B1 and Form TPC (EA) 1/96.

  1. Send education certificate to NA and sign the prepared contract.
  2. Complete B1 and TPC forms and return all documents.
  3. NA arranges signature and CTC by the Agency Principal.
  4. Submit returned soft copies through your BIS account.
  5. Collect two hard-copy sets and submit them physically to LPEPH.
  6. Follow up with LPEPH until approval.

REN reassignment · Active & inactive

Active REN

  1. Resign and follow up until detached from your current agency in the system.
  2. Inform NA once LPEPH approves the resignation.
  3. Sign the Contract for Service and provide the RM100 fee slip.
  4. NA submits via BIS and informs you after approval.

Inactive REN

Re-sit NCC, send the new certificate to NA, then return the signed contract, recent bankruptcy report and RM100 fee slip.

CIMB 8010632400 · Remark: Reassign fee
PEA reassignment
  1. Submit resignation through your personal BIS account.
  2. Email wan@lpeph.gov.my with the resignation letter and BIS screenshot.
  3. Sign the Contract for Service and complete Form TPC (EA) 3/96.
  4. Resubmit through BIS and email LPEPH to request approval.
  5. NA shares the business card link after approval.
REN renewal
Approved Jan–Sep: Attend RRP in the current year.
Approved Oct–Dec: Attend RRP the following year, subject to LPEPH policy.

Active: Attend RRP between May and December, send the certificate to NA, and wait for NA before generating the bankruptcy report.

Inactive: Re-sit NCC and complete the contract plus recent bankruptcy report. The RM100 fee only applies if LPEPH removed you from the agency BIS system.

PEA & REA renewal

PEA active: Complete 5 CPD hours—minimum 3 physical and 2 online, with no carry-forward. Submit through BIS after renewals open.

PEA inactive: Confirm fees with LPEPH, complete Forms D1 and F, and submit the CPD certificate, bankruptcy declaration and payment slip.

REA: Renew before 15 October. Below 55: 10 CPD hours, minimum 5 physical + 5 online. Above 55: 5 hours, minimum 3 physical + 2 online. Up to 5 carry-forward hours are allowed.

PDPA: immediate actions
  • Delete prospect data immediately after an opt-out request and do not contact them again.
  • Include a clear way to unsubscribe from marketing messages.
  • Obtain direct consent before adding a prospect to a broadcast list.
  • Never circulate prospect contact lists without explicit consent.
  • Password-protect devices and limit data collection to what is necessary.

Quick answers

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I become a Certified Real Estate Negotiator?
You must be at least 18, hold an accepted minimum education qualification, meet the insolvency requirements, complete NCC and be attached to a licensed agency. Non-Malaysians require an accepted visa and correct work permit.
Where can I complete NCC?
Choose an association officially appointed by LPEPH. The two-day programme fee stated in this guide is RM648 inclusive of 8% SST.
When can I get my business card?
After your active registration and reassignment are approved and you have completed the official corporate photoshoot.
Can SPM Bahagian 3 or SAP be used?
No. LPEPH accepts SPM Bahagian 1 or 2. Ask an APEL provider about possible alternative qualification routes.
Can I get a refund if my education certificate cannot be used?
No. The RM388 joining fee is strictly non-refundable.
Can a bankrupt applicant apply as a REN?
Potentially, with supporting documents required by the Department of Insolvency. Final approval remains at LPEPH’s discretion. Contact NA for the current document list.
What should I complete within my first 3 working days?
Join the required WhatsApp groups, review the Department Directory, complete and return the mandatory form, read the PDPA memo, and begin the relevant REN or PEA application process.
Who should I contact if I am unsure which application process applies to me?
Contact the Negotiator Affairs Department before submitting any documents. They will confirm the correct process based on your nationality and registration status.
When should I generate my Bankruptcy Report?
Only generate it after signing your Contract for Service or when the Negotiator Affairs Department instructs you to do so. LPEPH requires a recent copy.
How can I check the status of my REN application?
The Negotiator Affairs Department will notify you through WhatsApp after your application is submitted and when LPEPH provides an update or approval.
Is the corporate photoshoot compulsory?
Yes. Your official corporate photo is required for your system profile, business card, marketing materials and milestone recognition posters.
How do I submit the mandatory Acknowledgement & Indemnity Form?
Download the form, complete and sign it, then email the completed copy to hr@propnexmalaysia.net. Keep a copy for your own reference.
Which WhatsApp groups are compulsory to join?
At minimum, join the PropNex Announcement, Event & Training, and Negotiator Affairs Updates groups. Other groups may be joined based on your business focus.
What should I do if this Welcome Kit differs from the latest company announcement?
Always follow the latest official company announcement or memo. If you are unsure, verify with the relevant department before taking action.
